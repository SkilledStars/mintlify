## How it fits together

- A **campaign** belongs to a **brand** and targets one or more **audiences**.
- An audience is built from **data stores**: YouTube channels and videos, with their comments.
- Fandom generates **targeting** for each audience: keywords, segments, topics and websites.
- When it's ready, create the campaign in **Google Ads**. It starts paused.

Three more views help you find the right audience:

- **Audience Exploration** shows who an audience is on YouTube: the channels they also watch, and how those channels group.
- **Inclusion lists** rank channels for ads or sponsorship, grown from seed channels and checked against a brief.
- **Projects** map everyone around a set of starting channels, and hold their reports and lists.

MCP covers all of it. The API covers campaigns and everything around them, plus the basics of [Audience Exploration](/guides/exploration).
