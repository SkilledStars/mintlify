Talk to it like a colleague. Names are enough: your app looks up the ids itself.

## Explore an audience

- "Map the home espresso audience on YouTube. Who are they?"
- "Split that map by life stage."
- "In the specialty coffee group, which small channels have the highest fan density?"
- "What else do these fans subscribe to?"
- "Compare Nike and Adidas on this map."
- "Write an audience report and save it."
- "Save those channels as an audience called Espresso fans."

## Build an inclusion list

- "Build an inclusion list of 200 channels for espresso gear buyers, US only, at least 25K subscribers."
- "Is it ready? Show me the top 20 and anything flagged."
- "Why is this channel on the list?"
- "Give me the Google Ads file."
- "Raise the minimum to 100K subscribers and run it again."

Downloads come as the Google Ads placements file, the full list, or a workbook. Links last 24 hours.

## Work on a project

- "Start a project to understand the home espresso audience."
- "What do they say about grinders?"
- "What are they passionate about?"
- "Make the report, add a section on how often top channels post, and give me a link to share."
- "Make an inclusion list of the top 100 channels from this project."
- "Write a creator report on @jameshoffmann. Which brands could partner with this channel?"

## Run campaigns

- "What's in my workspace?"
- "Create a Demand Gen campaign for my brand using the Espresso fans audience, then show me the targeting."
- "Remove anything about travel from the targeting."
- "Create it in Google Ads."
- "How did my campaigns do over the last 30 days?"

Google Ads campaigns are always created paused.

## Good to know

- **Big jobs take a few minutes.** Reports and lists keep working while your app checks back. If it loses track, ask "is it ready?"
- **Several workspaces?** Say which one you mean.
- **People means people who comment,** not everyone who watches.
- **Your app asks before changing things.** Most apps show you each change first. Check yours if you want that on or off.
