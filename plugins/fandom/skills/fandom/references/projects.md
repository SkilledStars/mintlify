# A research project and a report to share

For "help me understand the audience of…", a client research brief, or a report someone will read.

1. **Plan it.** `propose_project` reads the brief into a plan: starting channels, the questions the data can answer, the ones only the research team can, and how to split the channels. It creates nothing.
2. **Ask, then build.** `create_project` starts a paid build (early results in about 20 seconds, the rest in minutes). Confirm the plan first.
3. **Read it first.** `get_project` lists what the project holds: its audiences, groups, reports and lists. Name selections exactly as it lists them.
4. **Answer questions.**
   - `get_project_answers` gives the numbers.
   - `ask_project_question` answers a question from the data. When it says only the research team can answer, offer `send_question_to_research_team`.
5. **Go deeper (paid, ask first).**
   - `run_project_lens` runs one lens: watching, passions, saying or how.
   - `run_project_content_analysis` reads formats, length and posting.
6. **Make the report.** `create_project_report` makes and fills a report for a selection. `update_project_report` adds or removes sections, and `share_project_report` freezes it into a link to share.
7. **Lists and watches.**
   - `create_project_inclusion_list` makes a list from part of the map (paid).
   - `create_trend` keeps a question on chosen channels, daily or weekly (spends AI each run).

Good answer: the headline finding, the three numbers that support it, the report link, and what to ask next.
